はいYes

Usage: The word **はい** is used to affirm or agree politely.

Kun'yomi: はい hai

Hiragana breakdown: は ha い i

Usage example: Used in responses like **はい、そうです** (hai, sou desu, “Yes, that’s right.”).

いいえNo

Usage: The word **いいえ** is used to negate or disagree politely.

Kun'yomi: いいえ iie

Hiragana breakdown: い i い i え e

Usage example: Used in responses like **いいえ、違います** (iie, chigaimasu, “No, that’s not correct.”).

すみませんExcuse me / sorry

Usage: The phrase **すみません** is used to apologize or get attention politely.

Kun'yomi: すみません sumimasen

Hiragana breakdown: す su み mi ま ma せ se ん n

Usage example: Used when apologizing or asking for help, e.g. **すみません、トイレはどこですか** (sumimasen, toire wa doko desu ka, “Excuse me, where is the toilet?”).

ごめんなさいSorry

Usage: The phrase **ごめんなさい** is a stronger apology than すみません。

Kun'yomi: ごめんなさい gomen nasai

Hiragana breakdown: ご go め me ん n な na さ sa い i

Usage example: Used when apologizing for a mistake, e.g. **ごめんなさい、遅れました** (gomen nasai, okuremashita, “Sorry, I’m late.”).

Usage: The word **ありがとう** expresses gratitude casually.

Hiragana breakdown: あ a り ri が ga と to う u
